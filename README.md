## Featured Projects

### 01 / K-NEXUS
**Financial Intelligence & Context-Aware Fraud Defense**

![KBTG](https://img.shields.io/badge/KBTG_Kampus_Hackathon-2026-138477?style=flat-square)
![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-FinTech-213C49?style=flat-square)
![Focus](https://img.shields.io/badge/Focus-Graph_ML-213C49?style=flat-square)

An independent, K PLUS-inspired financial intelligence prototype designed to address two challenges faced by young professionals: **cashflow uncertainty and digital transaction fraud**.

The system combines predictive cashflow intelligence with graph-based fraud risk assessment.

#### FlowSense — Cashflow Intelligence

- **30-Day Liquidity Forecasting:** Uses transaction patterns and predictive modeling to estimate future cashflow conditions.
- **Daily Spending Guidance:** Translates financial projections into actionable spending insights.
- **Budget Simulation:** Helps users understand how planned expenses affect their financial runway.
- **User-Controlled Savings:** Supports intentional savings decisions with transparent controls.

#### TrustGraph — Fraud Risk Intelligence

- **Graph-Based Risk Detection:** Uses relational transaction intelligence to identify suspicious account relationships.
- **Context-Aware Risk Assessment:** Evaluates transfer risk before a transaction proceeds.
- **Explainable Risk Reasoning:** Presents understandable explanations instead of opaque risk scores.
- **Adaptive Verification:** Applies risk-sensitive checks and configurable review periods for suspicious transfers.

**Technical Highlights**

- Graph intelligence built around **R-GCN / PyTorch Geometric**.
- Predictive modeling with **LightGBM**.
- Low-latency inference using **ONNX-based serving**.
- Reported prototype benchmark: **P99 11.62 ms**, compared with an 80 ms latency target.
- Full-stack application with **FastAPI and React**.

**Technology Stack**

`Python` `PyTorch Geometric` `R-GCN` `LightGBM` `ONNX Runtime` `FastAPI` `React`

### 02 / Shopee COD Reliability Engine
**Predictive Risk Scoring & Logistics Intelligence**

![Competition](https://img.shields.io/badge/Shopee_Ultimate_Case_Challenge-2026-EE4D2D?style=flat-square)
![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-E--Commerce-213C49?style=flat-square)
![Focus](https://img.shields.io/badge/Focus-Risk_Modeling-213C49?style=flat-square)

An end-to-end machine learning decision-support system designed to predict **Cash on Delivery (COD) failures** and support more reliable e-commerce logistics decisions.

The project combines predictive risk modeling, explainable scoring, and adaptive buyer policies.

**Technical Highlights**

- Developed a calibrated **Random Forest** risk classification pipeline.
- Achieved a reported **ROC-AUC of 0.8629** on the project's evaluation dataset.
- Transformed failure probabilities into interpretable buyer reliability scores.
- Designed a **four-tier progressive risk policy** with dynamic buyer feedback.
- Built an interactive checkout simulator and risk analytics dashboard.
- Evaluated potential business impact using simulation-based scenarios.

**Technology Stack**

`Python` `Scikit-learn` `Pandas` `SciPy` `FastAPI` `JavaScript`

**Project Links**

[![Source Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/Source_Code-GitHub-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/svkhun/Shopee-Ultimate-Case-Challenge)
